### Омлет с кабачком и сыром (`cur_zucchini_cheese_omelette`)

**Published method**: [original source](https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/herbed-cheese-and-zucchini-omelet-479). The published herbed cheese and zucchini omelet softens zucchini in a hot skillet, makes a beaten egg omelet, uses grated cheddar cheese, and finishes the folded omelet under a lid until set.

**Source-to-FoodPlan differences**: FoodPlan softens grated zucchini, keeps it in the skillet and pours beaten whole eggs over it; its source removes zucchini, separately whips egg whites and folds the omelet. Butter and herb cheeses are replaced with sunflower oil and Gouda. Exact texture/time are not transferred.

**Raw weights**: egg 125 g, zucchini 160 g, cheese 45 g, oil 6 g.
